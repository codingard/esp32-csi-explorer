Connect your receiver

Flash the pinned Espressif csi_recv firmware, close other serial monitors, then select Connect USB in desktop Chrome or Edge. Data stays in this browser.

Open amplitude CSV, ESP-IDF CSI CSV or compatible JSON to inspect saved measurements. The data surface shows sample index, CSI bin and magnitude; it does not estimate body shape or location.

Playback speed is a display control, separate from the acquisition rate. Bundled sample timing is unavailable.
